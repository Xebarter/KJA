### DESIGN DIRECTION: KJA PREMIUM REPORT CARD

Design a premium, professionally typeset school report card inspired by the Clevers' Origin school uniform. The visual identity must reflect the uniform's distinctive charcoal grey, black, white and deep burgundy-red check pattern.

**1. Colour palette**
- Deep burgundy red as the primary accent colour.
- Charcoal grey and near-black for strong headings and text.
- White as the dominant background.
- Light grey for subtle section backgrounds, table divisions and supporting elements.

**2. Visual identity**
Use the school's uniform pattern as a subtle design element. Introduce carefully controlled plaid/checkered detailing using charcoal, black, white and thin burgundy-red lines. The pattern can appear as a narrow header strip, footer accent, section divider or a small decorative panel. Avoid using large checkered backgrounds that interfere with readability.

**3. Header design**
Create an elegant report card header with the school logo, school name, campus details, academic year and term. Use a deep burgundy accent line and subtle plaid detailing to establish the school's identity. The header should look formal, prestigious and well-organised.

**4. Typography**
Use modern, professional typography. School and section headings should be bold and distinctive, while learner information, assessment details and teacher comments should use highly legible fonts. Use burgundy for important labels and charcoal for primary text.

**5. Academic tables**
Create clean, structured academic tables with:
- Clear column headings.
- Subtle grey row divisions.
- Burgundy highlights for important scores, grades and performance indicators.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Strong readability when printed.

**6. Report sections**
Use visually distinct but consistent sections for learner particulars, academic performance, co-curricular activities, teacher comments, attendance, conduct, overall performance and promotion recommendations.

**7. Premium finishing**
Introduce restrained burgundy borders, thin charcoal dividers, small plaid accents and carefully spaced information blocks. Use the uniform's shield/badge identity as inspiration for small report-card labels and achievement indicators.

**8. Printing requirements**
The report card must remain predominantly white to minimise printing costs. It must look excellent when printed on ordinary A4 paper in both colour and black and white. Avoid gradients, excessive decoration, heavy background fills and unnecessary graphical elements.

**Overall aesthetic:** Premium British-inspired school stationery, modern academic editorial design, sophisticated, structured, elegant and distinctly recognisable as Clevers' Origin Schools.

Maintain the existing report card's data structure, assessment calculations, grading logic and functionality. This is a visual redesign, not a change to the reporting system.
